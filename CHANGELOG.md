# Changelog

## 1.0 — October 2026

First release.

- Full notes, shortened notes, and bibliography entries in the notes-and-bibliography style of *The Chicago Manual of Style*, 18th edition. It covers 15 kinds of source:
  - books, including edited and translated books
  - chapters in edited books
  - journal, magazine, and newspaper articles
  - web pages and blog posts
  - reports and government documents
  - dissertations and theses
  - conference papers and talks
  - online videos
  - social media posts
  - archival documents and letters
  - dictionary and encyclopedia entries
  - AI-generated content
- Follows the 18th-edition changes:
  - no place of publication
  - shortened notes in place of *ibid.*
  - a repeated author's name repeated in the bibliography
  - notes-only sources left out of the bibliography
- Locators for pages, paragraphs, sections, chapters, and volumes, plus "quoted in" and several sources in one note.
- Headline-style titles and automatic short titles. You can type your own short title.
- Checks for common mistakes:
  - first-name-first authors
  - library database links
  - missing volumes, URLs, editors, and access dates
  - over-long short titles
  - quotations without a page
- A bibliography that sorts itself. Copy it, export it to Word or Markdown, or save the list to a file and open it again later.
- Runs entirely in the browser, and nothing is sent anywhere. Light and dark themes.
